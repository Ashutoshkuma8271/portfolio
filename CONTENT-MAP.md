# What content belongs on which page

**Rule of thumb:** a visitor should never have to guess where something lives.
Every fact has **one home**. Other pages may *point* to it (a one-line teaser + link), never repeat it.

| Page | It answers | Put here | Keep OFF this page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Home** | "Who is this, what does she do, where do I go next?" (7 seconds) | Hero (name · role · one-liner · **one** gold button + **one** text link · the 4-step path) · partner strip · 3-line intro + quote · **Four Pillars** (one card per section, each linking to its page) · **Invest With Us** (5 sectors) · **Live GCC Desk** (headlines + My View) · **Spotlight** (1 keynote + 2 latest press) · **Latest Essays** (3) · closing call to action | Full lists, long paragraphs, forms, statistics tables. Home summarises and points. |
| **About** | "Who is she?" | Biography (lead paragraph + "Read the full biography") · international roles · career timeline · honours · core tenets · portrait · one link to the press gallery | News, investment offers, media coverage, contact forms |
| **Trade & Investment** | "What can she do for my business or capital?" | Strategic corridors · live gold price (market desk) · advisory services · market-entry process · trade inquiry form | Press articles, videos, biography, the news feed (it lives on Home; this page links to it) |
| **Leadership** (Women Cell) | "What is the Women Cell doing?" | Mission quote · impact figures · programs · events (upcoming / completed) · join form | Trade content, personal biography |
| **Media & Press** | "Where has she been covered, seen and heard?" | Press features · **Watch** (her videos and reels) · speaking engagements · event photo gallery (with category filter) · media enquiry form | Essays she wrote, investment pitches, the media-kit download (removed) |
| **Insights** | "What does she think and write?" | Her essays, filtered by category · one featured essay | Press *about* her (that is Media), live news (that is the Desk) |
| **Contact** | "How do I reach the right person?" | Three desks (general · trade · press) · WhatsApp · offices and hours · **one** contact form | Marketing copy, statistics, second inquiry forms |

## Two tiers of contact (so forms don't multiply)
- **Tier 1 — quick touchpoint:** the pop-ups (*Invest With Us*, *Collaborate*, *Media enquiry*). Short, sector-aware.
- **Tier 2 — official desk:** the Contact page form, plus the three long forms on Trade, Media and Leadership.
  Same field style everywhere; each goes to the right desk.

## Duplications removed (checked with an automatic scan of all 7 pages)
- Home: the second "Focus Pillars" card (the Four Pillars section is the one home) · the extra slogan line under the partner flags · the stats band.
- About: banner figures that belong to Trade (₹3,750+ Cr) and Leadership (50,000+ women) — the banner now shows facts about her (international roles, career milestones, honours) counted from the page's own data · the event gallery (**one home: Media & Press**; About links to it).
- Trade: the ₹3,750+ Cr tile inside the gold panel (it is in the Trade banner) · live news (one home: Home, `#live-desk`; Trade links to it).
- Leadership: "50,000 women" no longer repeated in the banner text and the heading below it.
- Contact: the banner rail repeating the offices listed below it.
- Media: the Media Kit section · the "Broadcast Dialogues" block. The photo gallery gained the category filter that used to live on About.
- Floating "Invest With Us" pill: hides while the Invest section itself is on screen.

## Writing rule for every card
Two lines: **what it is** + **why it matters**. Anything longer goes behind a "Read more" link — never delete it.
